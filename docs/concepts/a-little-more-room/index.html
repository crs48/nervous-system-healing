01 / A small beginning

What would be useful right now?

No need to name your state.
Just choose a direction.

Choose a kind of beginning

a.

An invitation · a few moments

Let an ordinary thing be ordinary.

If you like, find an object near you: a cup, a window, a fold in a blanket. Notice one shape or color. You don’t need to feel anything in particular.

Looking around doesn’t suit everyone. You can skip this, move, or just read.

More about orienting →
b.

An invitation · your choice of pace

Change one small thing.

If moving is comfortable for you, try shifting your position or moving a hand in a way you already know feels okay. Small is plenty. There’s no particular sensation to find.

Stay within a comfortable range. Skip anything painful, dizzying, or unwelcome.

c.

No exercise needed

You can start with a little context.

The internet has a lot of vocabulary for how we feel. You don’t need to learn it all before you begin. Start with the three ideas below, or take the existing introductory tour.

d.

You can bring someone in

Support belongs in the picture.

A trusted person, an accommodating environment, or a qualified clinician may be more useful than another exercise. If symptoms are persistent or disrupting daily life, a clinician can help you explore what’s going on.

For people exploring PTSD care, this public guide explains treatment options. This website provides education, not assessment or treatment.

That can be enough.

No next step required. You can close this page, carry on with your day, or come back when you like.

These are optional everyday experiments, not tests or trauma treatment. Nothing you select is saved.

02 / A little context

Less fixing.
More getting to know yourself.

Take the beginner tour ↗
01

Calm isn’t a character grade.

You don’t need to turn every feeling into a project. There’s room here for energy, uncertainty, rest, and a perfectly ordinary Tuesday.

02

You get to have preferences.

Eyes open or closed. Moving or still. Body-focused or outward-looking. If an invitation doesn’t fit, changing it is allowed.

03

A map is still a map.

Terms like “fight,” “flight,” and “freeze” can start a conversation. A website can’t tell what your nervous system is doing from a couple of sliders.
